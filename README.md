========================================================

1. CORE PRODUCT IDEA

========================================================

The prototype solves the problem of industrial investors being forced to understand multiple approvals, departments, documents, dependencies, timelines, compliance requirements and government support schemes.

The system should behave like an intelligent layer sitting above the existing MAITRI ecosystem.

Instead of asking investors to manually discover every approval, MAITRI Intelligence should ask for the project's business information and automatically generate:

1. Applicable approvals

2. Approval rationale

3. Required documents

4. Document readiness

5. Approval dependencies

6. Parallelizable approvals

7. Timeline/SLA

8. Delay-risk prediction

9. Pending applicant actions

10. Compliance requirements

11. Potential government incentives

12. Regulatory Q&A with sources

13. Officer-side case prioritization

14. Government-level bottleneck analytics

Do not make this a simple CRUD portal.

The CORE DIFFERENTIATOR must be the intelligence engine.
